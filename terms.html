2. Official Rules — how a paid run works

  1. Connect a wallet on DogeOS Chikyu Testnet and pick an entry tier. You pay the tier's entry fee to the SheriffArcade smart contract (0xf24E5251ad3DcB4bB66C1d669d9c6E5191CFE49a). The fee you pay must equal the tier's fee exactly; the contract and the server both check it.
  2. Play one run of Pixel Gulch. To win you must reach the end of the level and beat the High Noon boss (best-of-3 quick-draw).
  3. If you win, our server checks the run and posts the result on-chain. The contract computes the reward. Nothing is paid until you sign acceptReward with the exact terms shown.
  4. If you die, quit, restart, leave the page or run out of time, the run fails. Your entry fee stays in the prize pool. There are no refunds on failed runs. You sign acknowledgeForfeit to confirm; unsigned unfinished runs forfeit automatically after 30 minutes.
  5. One unsigned run per wallet: you can't start a new paid game until your last result is signed.

4. How rewards are calculated

Only a run that beats the boss earns a reward. Before the tier multiplier:

formula = 0.5 base + 0.03 per orb collected (30 in the level) + time bonus − 0.2 per hit taken, limited to at least 0.5 and at most 2.
Time bonus: 0.6 if you finish within 2:00, fading evenly to 0 at 5:00.

winnings = formula × tier multiplier
house fee = 1% of winnings
you receive = winnings − house fee

Example (Sheriff tier ×2): 20 orbs, 3:00, 1 hit → formula 1.3 → winnings 2.6 → fee 0.026 → you receive 2.574 test DOGE.

The 1% fee on winnings is taken only from a win. A failed run costs only its entry fee. The fee is sent to the operator's fee address. Results the server can't verify (impossible times, orb or hit counts) earn nothing; all limits are also enforced by the contract.

5. Odds and risk

This is a game of skill, not a lottery or a game of chance: there are no fixed odds, and whether you win depends on how you play. You can lose your whole entry fee on any run. Higher tiers are harder, so you are more likely to lose. Testnet history before tiers: 4 of 23 paid runs beat the boss (mostly our own test runs). Rewards depend on the prize pool having funds; the contract reserves the maximum payout of every open run, so a win that was posted can always be paid.

6. Responsible play

  • Only play with what you can afford to lose. Never chase losses.
  • Limits: at most 20 paid entries per wallet per day, 10 rewarded runs per wallet per day, a daily prize budget, and a maximum single payout of 10 test DOGE.
  • You can set your own daily limit on the page ("Set my daily limit"). It's stored on your device.
  • If gaming stops being fun, take a break. In the US you can call or text 1-800-GAMBLER or visit ncpgambling.org; elsewhere, look for a local support service.
